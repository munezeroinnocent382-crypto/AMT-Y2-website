What is Agricultural Mechanization Technology?

Agricultural Mechanization Technology focuses on the application of engineering, machinery and technology in agricultural production.

Our studies help us understand agricultural machines, tractors, farm implements, irrigation systems, maintenance, soil preparation and modern farming technologies.
